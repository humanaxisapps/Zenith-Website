---
title: "Strict limits come to Zenith"
---

![Zenith app blocker and app limits for iPhones and iPads with strict limit](/app_view/release_notes/2026-07-14.png)

We've added one of our most requested features: **Strict Limits**.

A video recently went viral of an engineer building a tool that showed a cat animation on his girlfriend's computer whenever she spent too long on it. While we don't have a cat, we took some inspiration from the idea and brought a similar concept to Zenith.

With Strict Limits, you can set **how many sessions you want each day and how long each session can be**. Once you've used your sessions for the day, the limit locks until the next day.
