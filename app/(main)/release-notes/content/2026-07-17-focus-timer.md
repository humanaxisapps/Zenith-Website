title: "Zenith adds a focus timer"
---

![Zenith Pomodoro timer for iPhones and iPads](/app_view/release_notes/2026-07-17.png)

I reconnected with a journalist I used to work with, and we got talking about focus and getting into the zone. She introduced me to something called a Pomodoro timer she uses at work.

The Pomodoro Technique (Pomodoro is Italian for tomato 🍅) is a popular time-management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. It breaks work into focused intervals with short breaks to help combat procrastination and mental fatigue.

We've now added a Pomodoro timer to Zenith to help you stay focused while studying, doing deep work, or working towards your goals.

Give it a try and get into the zone.
