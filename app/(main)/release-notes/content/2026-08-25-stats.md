title: "Track your app usage"
---

![Zenith can help you monitor app usage on iPhones and iPads](/app_view/release_notes/2026-08-25.png)

One of our users asked if they could see their app usage stats directly in Zenith, so we got to work.

Zenith can now show you how much time you're spending across the apps you use.

**Your data stays on your device.** Zenith uses Apple's Screen Time API to retrieve your app usage, and your personal usage data never leaves your phone.
