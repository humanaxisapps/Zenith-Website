After a year and a half of building, **Zenith is now live!**

Zenith helps you take back control of your time. Set limits on distracting apps, create goals, build better habits, and make more time for what matters most, whether that’s learning a new skill, nurturing relationships, doing focused deep work, or simply spending more time on the things you care about.

## Story behind the build

Zenith has been a project built across two countries, **Australia and Vietnam**, by the two friends Shawn Frank and Nguyễn Xuân Hanh.

Our mission is simple: **help people reach their Zenith** — their highest point.

Going live is just the beginning. We’ll keep building and adding new features to help you take back your time, reduce distractions, build better habits, and focus on what matters.

We’re incredibly excited to finally share Zenith with the world. **This is just the start.**
