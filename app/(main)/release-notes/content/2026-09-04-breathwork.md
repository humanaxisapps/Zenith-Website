title: "Breathe with Zenith"
---

![Zenith restores your calm, focus, and balance with breathwork](/app_view/release_notes/2026-09-04.png)

I was speaking with a yoga instructor about Zenith and what we're trying to achieve. She shared an insight that stuck with me: we often look outward for solutions to anxiety and focus, when sometimes simply taking a moment to breathe can make a difference.

That conversation inspired us to add **Breathwork** to Zenith, giving you simple breathing practices to help restore calm, focus, and balance.

You'll find Breathwork in the **Thrive library**. Give it a try and let us know what you think.
